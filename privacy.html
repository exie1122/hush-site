Hush

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

In short:

  • Hush has no accounts of its own, and no analytics, ads or trackers.
  • You log in on Instagram's own page. Your password goes to Instagram, never to Hush.
  • Your messages stay between your phone and Instagram. Notifications reach your phone encrypted, and we can't read them.
  • Signing out of an account in Hush deletes what Hush kept for it.

Who we are

Hush is made by Ethan Xie ("we"). You can reach us at ethan_xyz@hotmail.com.

Instagram

Hush shows Instagram's own website inside the app. When you log in, you type your username and password into Instagram's login page, and they go straight to Instagram. What you see and do there (your messages, stories, profile, who you follow) goes between your phone and Instagram, as it does in a web browser. Instagram's use of that information is covered by Instagram's Privacy Policy, not this one.

What stays on your phone

Hush keeps these on your phone only. We never receive them.

  • Your Instagram login session for each account you add (the website data a browser would keep).
  • Images of your inbox and profile from your last visit, shown while they load again, and your accounts' profile pictures.
  • The keys that decrypt your notifications, kept in the iOS keychain.
  • Your settings, your notification filters and the usernames you choose to always notify you.
  • The usernames and names of people whose messages notified you, used only to suggest names when you set up notification filters.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications, Hush uses a server of ours to deliver them to your iPhone. For this, the server stores your device's push notification token and a random identifier for each account, not your Instagram username, login or messages. Notifications pass through it encrypted, and only your phone can decrypt them.

The server is hosted in Canada. Our hosting provider and Apple's push notification service process this data only to deliver notifications. Like any server, ours sees your IP address when the app connects; its logs never contain message contents and are kept only briefly.

This data is deleted when you sign out of the account in Hush, or when notifications stop reaching your device (for example, after you delete Hush or turn notifications off).

Purchases

Hush+ is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your payment details. Hush only checks with Apple, on your phone, whether your subscription is active.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your information, and don't use it for advertising.
  • We don't use analytics, tracking or advertising tools, or anyone else's code that collects data.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.

Your choices

  • Sign out (Settings → Accounts) deletes an account's login session, cached images, notification keys and server record from Hush. Your Instagram account itself isn't changed.
  • Delete Hush to remove everything it kept on your phone. Our server's records for it are deleted once notifications stop reaching your device.
  • Turn notifications off in iOS Settings at any time.
  • To have your Instagram data deleted, use Instagram's own settings.

Children

Hush is for people old enough to use Instagram under Instagram's rules (13 or older, or older where local law requires). We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects you significantly, we'll mention it in the app.

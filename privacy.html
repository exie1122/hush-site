Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

In short:

  • Hush has no accounts of its own, and no analytics, ads or trackers.
  • You log in on Instagram's own page. Your password goes to Instagram, never to Hush.
  • Your messages stay between your phone and Instagram. Hush's notification server passes them on still encrypted and can't read them.
  • Signing out of an account in Hush deletes what Hush kept for it.

Who we are

Hush is made by Ethan Xie ("we"). You can reach us at ethan_xyz@hotmail.com.

Instagram

Hush shows Instagram's own website inside the app. When you log in, you type your username and password into Instagram's login page, and they go straight to Instagram. What you see and do there (your messages, stories, profile, who you follow) goes between your phone and Instagram, as it does in a web browser. Instagram's use of that information is covered by Instagram's Privacy Policy, not this one.

To notify you about new messages, Hush registers your phone with Instagram's web notifications, as a browser does. Instagram is given an address to send notifications to and the public keys that encrypt them.

What stays on your phone

Hush keeps these on your phone only. We never receive them.

  • Your Instagram login session for each account you add (the website data a browser would keep).
  • Images of your inbox and profile from your last visit, shown while they load again, and your accounts' profile pictures.
  • The keys that decrypt your notifications, kept in the iOS keychain.
  • Your settings, your notification filters and the usernames you choose to always notify you.
  • The usernames and names of people whose messages notified you, used only to suggest names when you set up notification filters.

Notifications

Instagram only sends web notifications to established push services, so Hush uses a small server of ours (the "relay") to pass them on to your iPhone:

  • Instagram encrypts each notification with your phone's keys and sends it to Mozilla's push service, which passes it to our relay. Mozilla's handling is covered by Mozilla's Privacy Notice.
  • The relay forwards it, still encrypted, to Apple's push service, which delivers it to your phone. Only your phone can decrypt it.

For each account on each phone, the relay stores your phone's Apple push token, the push channel it forwards, a random ID that Hush made for the account (not your Instagram username or ID), a hashed secret and the time it was set up. It doesn't receive your messages' contents, your keys, your Instagram login or your username.

The relay runs on Fly.io in Canada. Like any web server, it and its host see your phone's IP address when the app connects. Its logs record requests and errors, never message contents, and are kept only briefly.

The relay's record for an account is deleted when you sign out of that account in Hush, or when Apple reports that the app was removed or notifications were turned off for good.

Purchases

Hush+ is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your payment details. Hush only checks with Apple, on your phone, whether your subscription is active.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your information, and don't use it for advertising.
  • We don't use analytics, tracking or advertising tools, or anyone else's code that collects data.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.

Your choices

  • Sign out (Settings → Accounts) deletes an account's login session, cached images, notification keys and relay record from Hush. Your Instagram account itself isn't changed.
  • Delete Hush to remove everything it kept on your phone. The relay's records are deleted the next time Instagram tries to notify you there.
  • Turn notifications off in iOS Settings at any time.
  • To have your Instagram data deleted, use Instagram's own settings.

Children

Hush is for people old enough to use Instagram under Instagram's rules (13 or older, or older where local law requires). We don't knowingly collect information from children.
